# Data

`credit_card_default.csv.gz` is the **Default of Credit Card Clients** dataset:

> Yeh, I. (2009). Default of Credit Card Clients [Dataset]. UCI Machine Learning Repository.
> https://doi.org/10.24432/C55S3H

Licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Converted from the
original `.xls` without changing values. Column names were lower-cased, `PAY_0` was renamed
`pay_1` to match `bill_amt1` and `pay_amt1`, and the target was renamed `default_next_month`.

30,000 credit card accounts in Taiwan (April to September 2005): credit limit, sex, education,
marital status, age, six months of repayment status, statement balances and payments, and
whether the account defaulted the following month (22.1%).
